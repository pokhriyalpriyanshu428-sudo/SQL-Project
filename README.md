**Project Title: Swiggy Instamart FMCG Sales Analytics**

This project optimizes Swiggy Instamart’s dark-store operations across Delhi-NCR using advanced SQL. I engineered a relational schema to track snacks and beverages, focusing on inventory turnover and regional sales performance.

Technical Core:
I utilized CTEs and Window Functions (RANK, LAG) to calculate Month-over-Month growth and identify top-performing products. Complex queries performed Pareto Analysis to isolate the 20% of stores generating 80% of revenue.

Business Value:
The analysis identifies "Slow-Moving" stock, allowing for data-driven "Flash Deals" to reduce waste. It provides actionable insights into procurement, ensuring high-demand items remain in stock to beat competitors like Blinkit and Zepto.
